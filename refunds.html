Cancellation & Refund Policy

How refunds and cancellations will work for Vidyarthi programmes and third-party course purchases.

Third-party courses

Where Vidyarthi links to an external provider, the purchase is made under that provider’s checkout and refund policy. Vidyarthi does not control the provider’s refund decision.

Future Vidyarthi programmes

For any future Vidyarthi-owned or authorised paid programme, the applicable price, cancellation window and refund conditions will be shown before payment and on the relevant programme page.

Institutional programmes

Institutional and government engagements are governed by the applicable proposal, purchase order, work order or agreement.

Contact
