About Vidyarthi

A learning platform built around real people, not course counts.

Vidyarthi.ai is a Golden Turtle initiative focused on practical AI, digital, technical, communication, employability and vocational learning for India's diverse learners.

The idea

Too much choice. Too little direction.

Vidyarthi brings learning into a simpler starting point: choose a goal, understand the skills required, then find the right learning route.

The platform is designed for students, early-career professionals, working adults, vocational learners, institutions and public skilling programmes.

Curated marketplace

Bring relevant learning from multiple providers into an organised discovery experience.

Free learning layer

Use public and genuinely free resources as a trust-building entry point, subject to rights.

AI guidance layer

Vidyarthi helps learners decide what to learn before they commit to a course.
