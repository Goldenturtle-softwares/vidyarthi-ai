Pricing & Programme Information

Vidyarthi.ai combines free learning, third-party course discovery, future authorised programmes and institutional skilling.

Free learning

The Free Classroom and selected public learning resources are available without a Vidyarthi course fee, subject to the source platform’s terms.

Third-party courses

Where Vidyarthi links to a third-party course provider, the provider controls the current price, checkout and refund terms. Any affiliate relationship will be clearly identified internally in the catalogue system.

Vidyarthi programmes

When Vidyarthi launches its own or authorised paid programmes, the applicable price will be displayed clearly before checkout.

Institutions and government

College, employer and government programmes are generally customised. Contact founder@vidyarthi.ai or founder@vidyarthi.ai for programme discussions.
